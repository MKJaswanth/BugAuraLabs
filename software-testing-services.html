Questions

Before you share access.

A good testing engagement begins with clear boundaries. These are the questions founders and product teams usually ask first.

Can we sign an NDA first?

Yes. An NDA can be signed before credentials, documentation, customer data, or non-public product information are shared.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. BugAura Labs works remotely and supports asynchronous communication across time zones. Your preferred overlap hours are agreed during scoping.

Can you test a product that is still being built?

Yes. Early testing can focus on a staging environment, design acceptance criteria, individual workflows, APIs, or release candidates rather than waiting for the entire product.

What access is required?

Usually a test URL, role-based test accounts, known requirements, supported browsers or devices, and safe test data. Production access is not assumed or requested unless it is necessary and explicitly authorised.

Is security testing a penetration test?

No. Security-focused QA can identify common validation, authentication, authorisation, and information-exposure problems. Formal penetration testing requires a separately agreed specialist scope.
