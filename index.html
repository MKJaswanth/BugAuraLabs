FAQ

Questions clients usually ask before a QA sprint.

Clear answers remove friction before the first call.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. I can sign an NDA before reviewing staging links, admin panels, test data, internal docs, or unreleased product flows.

How long does a QA sprint take?

A focused audit can usually start with 1 to 3 priority flows. Full web app or ecommerce QA depends on scope, devices, browsers, and access.

Do you work with international teams?

Yes. BugAura Labs works remotely with founders, agencies, SaaS teams, and ecommerce brands across the US, UK, EU, UAE, Australia, and India.

What happens after bugs are fixed?

I retest fixed issues, check nearby flows for regressions, and update the report with pass, fail, blocked, or retest notes.

What access do you need?

Usually a staging URL, test account, admin access when needed, test payment mode, expected rules, and any known risk areas.

What if I only need a quick review?

The free QA audit is built for that. Send one important flow and I will return the top visible risks and the recommended next scope.
